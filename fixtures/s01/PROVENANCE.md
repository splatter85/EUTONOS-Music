## Reproduction and identity

The authoritative construction is `scripts/generate-fixtures.ts`, generator
version **1**, seed **0x454d4601** (a fixed xorshift32 noise seed). Run
`npm run fixtures:generate` to regenerate the six WAV files and `manifest.json`.
Run `npm run fixtures:check` to compare every generated byte without writing.
The manifest records each file's exact SHA-256 digest, byte count, PCM frame
count, channel count, and sample rate. Documentation files are maintained by
hand and are not overwritten by the generator.

All synthesis uses integer sample arithmetic, a fixed Q24 phase accumulator,
explicitly rounded pitch increments, integer envelopes, and seeded integer
noise. WAV fields and PCM samples are written explicitly in little-endian
order. No system clock, entropy source, locale, network service, installed
instrument, or operating-system audio device enters generation. Products of
integers stay within JavaScript's exact integer range; divisions use explicit
truncation. Output is 24,000 Hz, mono, signed 16-bit PCM in RIFF/WAVE containers.

## Musical and timing intent

Recordings A and B are two different, finite, eight-bar arrangements at 120 BPM
in 4/4. Each lasts exactly 16 seconds / 384,000 frames. A has a slower triangle
bass pulse; B has syncopated drums and bass, a smoothed pulse bass voice, and a
higher chord voicing. The pitched material stays on A, C, and E throughout;
there is no changing chord progression to conceal beneath a key label.

The entry is quarter 0, the transition exit is quarter 16, and the finite end
is quarter 32. The declared A-to-B route overlaps 8 quarters, leaving sufficient
recorded material after A's exit. Four-bar phrase boundaries are explicit. The
recordings do not loop and do not include an embedded player or generator.

The four separate kick, percussion, A2 bass, and A minor harmony samples are
original inputs for future procedural work. A2 bass has MIDI root 45. The
harmony sample contains A2/C3/E3; its declared root is A2 (MIDI 45). The recipe
reference is metadata only and makes no claim of procedural playback.
